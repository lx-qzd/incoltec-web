In bakery, colours must survive baking; in confectionery, sugar cooking, panning and long shelf exposure. We recommend the colour according to the process temperature and when it is added.

## What to consider

- **Baking:** curcumin, carmine, beta-carotene and vegetable carbon keep their colour well under heat.
- **Golden doughs and pastries:** saffron and beta-carotene give golden shades.
- **Gummies and jellies:** spirulina gives blues and greens when added at the end of cooking, once the temperature drops.
- **Dragées, chewing gum and white coatings:** LINICOL CF White is an alternative to titanium dioxide (E171).
- **Black doughs:** vegetable carbon gives deep, stable blacks.

## Typical applications

Speciality bread, pastries, biscuits, fillings and glazes, hard candy, gummies, chewing gum, dragées and chocolate or sugar coatings.

## How we work

We test the colour in your process (temperatures, times, pH) to set the dosage and avoid shade changes.
