In beverages, colour is the first thing consumers judge, and it stays visible through the pack for the whole shelf life. That is why we choose the colour according to each drink's **pH**, **heat treatment** and **light exposure**.

## What to consider

- **Acidic pH (2.5–4.0):** anthocyanins and black carrot, radish or hibiscus colouring foods give stable reds and raspberry shades in this range.
- **Pasteurisation and UHT:** carotenoids such as beta-carotene give oranges and yellows with good heat resistance.
- **Clear packaging:** some pigments fade in light; we adjust the formulation or recommend UV protection.
- **Clarity:** for clear drinks we use water-soluble versions or transparent emulsions.

## Typical applications

Soft and carbonated drinks, juices and nectars, sports and isotonic drinks, functional drinks, liqueurs and spirits.

## How we work

Our lab tests the colour in your actual matrix and recommends the dosage, the format (liquid or powder) and the regulatory documents for your market.
