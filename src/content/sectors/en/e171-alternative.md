Titanium dioxide (E171) is no longer authorised as a food additive in the European Union under Regulation (EU) 2022/63, following EFSA's 2021 assessment. Manufacturers who used it to whiten or opacify need an alternative that works in their process.

## What to consider

- **Opacity:** no alternative matches E171 exactly; dosage or the number of panning layers usually needs adjusting.
- **pH:** calcium carbonate bases are not suitable in very acidic media.
- **Process:** we check stability to baking or cooking so the white does not turn yellow.
- **Labelling:** we advise how to declare the alternative in the ingredient list.

## Typical applications

Dragées and sugar-coated sweets, chewing gum, white coatings and glazes, sauces and mayonnaise, bases for bright colours.

## How we work

We compare LINICOL CF White with your current formula and propose the dosage and process to get close to your previous result.
