Dairy products need appealing, consistent colours in matrices with protein, fat and often a mildly acidic pH. We choose the colour according to the product, the process and the label declaration you want.

## What to consider

- **Fruit yoghurts and desserts:** carmine gives very stable pinks and reds; black carrot and beetroot are plant-based alternatives.
- **Cheese and butter:** annatto (bixin and norbixin) and beta-carotene give the traditional yellows and oranges.
- **Ice cream:** spirulina enables natural blues and greens in products that are not heated strongly after it is added.
- **Heat treatment:** beetroot is heat-sensitive, so we recommend adding it cold or choosing another option for heat-treated products.

## Typical applications

Yoghurts and fermented milks, dairy desserts, milkshakes, fresh and aged cheeses, butter and margarine, ice cream and toppings.
