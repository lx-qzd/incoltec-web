In meat products, colour signals freshness and must hold through cooking, curing or chilled display. We also help recreate the look of meat in plant-based products.

## What to consider

- **Cured sausages and marinades:** paprika extract (oleoresin, E-160c), standardised in ASTA units, gives stable orange-reds.
- **Cooked meats:** carmine withstands cooking and sterilisation well.
- **Fresh products and plant-based alternatives:** beetroot mimics the red of raw meat; it is heat-sensitive, which can be used to imitate the colour change during cooking.
- **Coatings and marinades:** annatto and tomato powder add warm shades and flavour.

## Typical applications

Chorizo and cured sausages, cooked ham and cold cuts, sausages, marinades, coatings, burgers and plant-based alternatives.

## How we work

We adapt the colour to your process (curing, cooking, packaging) and provide the documentation for each market.
