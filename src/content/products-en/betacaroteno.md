**Natural Beta-carotene 10%** is our flagship solution for replacing synthetic yellows and oranges. It is carefully extracted and standardised to guarantee consistent colouring strength from batch to batch.

## Main applications

* **Soft drinks:** a bright orange that is stable to light and temperature changes.
* **Dairy and ice cream:** ideal for butter, cheese and dairy desserts, reproducing rich, natural-looking colours.
* **Bakery:** a perfect golden tone for sweet baked goods.

## Key advantages

1. **Natural origin:** a plant-derived alternative to synthetic colours (declared as E-160a).
2. **High stability:** formulated to withstand pasteurisation and UHT.
3. **Versatility:** available as liquid emulsions and microencapsulated powder.
