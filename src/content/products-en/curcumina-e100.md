INCOLTEC's **Curcumin E-100** is one of the most versatile and in-demand natural colours in the international food industry. Formulated to replace synthetic colours such as Tartrazine (E-102), it gives a warm, luminous yellow.

## Main applications

* **Confectionery and pastry:** bright golden shades in sweets, creams and glazes.
* **Sauces and condiments:** the reference for mustard, mayonnaise and emulsified sauces.
* **Snacks and extruded products:** strong resistance to heat in corn and wheat snacks.
* **Soft drinks:** stable water-soluble emulsions that do not settle.

## Key advantages

1. **Rigorous standardisation:** spectrophotometric control of every batch for consistent colouring strength.
2. **Versatile formats:** liquid solutions, clear emulsions or micronised powder.
3. **Heat resistance:** formulated to withstand baking and pasteurisation.
