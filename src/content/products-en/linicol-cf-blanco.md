Since titanium dioxide (E171) was withdrawn as a food additive in the European Union (Regulation (EU) 2022/63, following the EFSA safety assessment), **LINICOL CF White** offers a practical replacement. Based on starches and calcium carbonate, it provides a strong whitening and opacifying effect.

## Main applications

* **Dragées and chewing gum:** white coatings that serve as a base for bright colours.
* **Sauces and mayonnaise:** improves appearance with creaminess and opacity.
* **Bakery glazes:** ideal for doughnut and cake coatings that need a bright white.

## Key advantages

1. **Regulatory fit:** designed for formulations that must comply with EU rules after the E171 withdrawal.
2. **High opacity:** designed to approach the light reflectance of titanium dioxide.
3. **Heat stability:** withstands baking and cooking without yellowing.
