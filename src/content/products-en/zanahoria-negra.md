**Black Carrot Concentrate** is a preferred choice for global manufacturers seeking a clean label without E-numbers. It is obtained by physical processes: pressing, filtration and concentration.

## Main applications

* **Soft drinks and fruit beers:** high brilliance and optical clarity.
* **Fruit yoghurts and ice cream:** a good alternative to carmine for vegetarian and vegan markets.
* **Jams and bakery fillings:** stable at the acidity of natural fruit.

## Key advantages

1. **Clean label:** classified as a food ingredient (colouring food), not as an additive.
2. **No animal-derived ingredients:** suitable for vegetarian and vegan positioning.
3. **Concentrated colouring power:** low dosages with high colour intensity.
