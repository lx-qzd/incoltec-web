Our **Beetroot Red E-162** is obtained from selected *Beta vulgaris* roots and gives an intense, vibrant red for many food applications. It is a preferred choice for manufacturers replacing artificial colours.

## Main applications

* **Meat products and sausages:** restores a natural red in sausages and processed meats.
* **Confectionery and gummies:** an attractive raspberry or strawberry colour.
* **Strawberry yoghurt:** the industry standard for a natural pink.

## Key advantages

1. **Natural origin:** obtained from beetroot.
2. **Excellent at acidic pH:** reaches its best colour in acidic media (pH 4–5).
3. **Neutral flavour:** our purification process removes beetroot's typical earthy notes.
