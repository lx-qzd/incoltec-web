INCOLTEC's **Vitamin and Micronutrient Premixes** combine nutrition science with homogeneous formulation technology. We supply precise formulations designed to meet target intakes and labelling claims.

## Main applications

* **Functional drinks and juices:** enrichment with vitamin C, B-complex vitamins and zinc.
* **Cereals and nutritional snacks:** microencapsulated ingredients that withstand toasting and extrusion.
* **Dairy and plant-based drinks:** fortification with vitamins A, D3, calcium and B12.

## Key advantages

1. **Precise dosing:** high-precision blending for uniform distribution.
2. **Process stability:** microencapsulation to limit degradation from oxygen or heat.
3. **Colour synergy:** colour and vitamins can be combined in a single reference.
