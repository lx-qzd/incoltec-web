INCOLTEC's **Copper Chlorophyllin E-141** solves the long-standing instability of ordinary plant chlorophyll to light and heat, giving a fresh, vibrant and long-lasting green.

## Main applications

* **Mint chewing gum and sweets:** refreshing, long-lasting greens.
* **Pistachio and mint ice cream:** an even green without grassy off-notes.
* **Isotonic drinks and juices:** stable in clear, bottled acidic drinks.

## Key advantages

1. **Superior light stability:** holds up under supermarket lighting.
2. **Wide pH range:** keeps its shade in alkaline and mildly acidic media.
3. **Tailored formats:** water- or oil-soluble versions depending on the food matrix.
