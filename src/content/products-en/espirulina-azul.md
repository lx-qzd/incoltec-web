A natural blue is one of the biggest technical milestones in food colour. INCOLTEC offers standardised **Spirulina Blue** and **Blue Genipa (Jagua/Huito)** concentrates to formulate both vibrant blues and pure greens (combined with curcumin or safflower).

## Main applications

* **Gummies and jelly sweets:** bright blues and greens without Brilliant Blue FCF (E-133).
* **Functional drinks and cocktails:** crystal-clear colour in chilled liquids.
* **Ice cream and children's confectionery:** well accepted by health-conscious consumers.

## Key advantages

1. **No synthetic blue:** a natural alternative to synthetic blue colours.
2. **Gentle extraction:** free from organic solvents.
3. **Blending versatility:** an ideal base for natural greens, turquoises and violets.
