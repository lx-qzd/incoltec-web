**Cochineal Carmine E-120** is recognised internationally for its resistance to heat degradation, sterilisation and prolonged light exposure.

## Main applications

* **Meat industry:** cooked ham, sausages, surimi and cured sausages. Keeps an appetising colour even after long periods in chilled displays.
* **Dairy:** strawberry yoghurts, milkshakes and dairy desserts in vivid pink shades.
* **Beverages and snacks:** stable to UV light and acidity.

## Key advantages

1. **Excellent heat resistance:** withstands retorting, UHT and demanding industrial cooking.
2. **Range of shades:** from bright crimson to deep purple depending on pH and formulation.
3. **Reliable supply:** consistent stock under international quality standards.
